Puzzle proofs

Solve grid puzzles as proofs. Every mark needs a reason, and the reason is always one of the game's own rules. Pick a game.

Game rules
    Proof controls
    • Every mark needs a reason. Click the clue that is your reason. The cells that rule talks about get a dashed outline.
    • Some cells and labels carry more than one rule. Click the same place again to move to its next rule.
    • Then mark an outlined cell. "Mark as" shows what the left and right mouse buttons place. The step is accepted only if that one rule forces it. Esc drops the cited clue.
    • The game applies the tedious consequences for you. The rules list says which ones.
    • Undo takes back one action at a time.
    • Assume opens an assumption, shown in purple. If a rule becomes impossible inside it, click that clue and press "This rule is broken". The assumption closes and its opposite is proved.
    • Case split and "Cases from this rule" open cases side by side. Click a case to work in it. A contradiction closes a case. "End cases" keeps whatever is true in every open case.
